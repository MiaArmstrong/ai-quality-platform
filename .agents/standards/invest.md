# INVEST

Use during Story / New Feature / Improvement readiness review.

| Letter | Meaning | Question |
|---|---|---|
| I | Independent | Can it be delivered without waiting on another same-sprint story? |
| N | Negotiable | Does it express intent rather than unnecessarily prescribing implementation? |
| V | Valuable | Is there a clear user/business benefit? |
| E | Estimable | Is enough known to estimate the work? |
| S | Small | Is it plausibly deliverable within one sprint? |
| T | Testable | Can at least one objective acceptance test be derived? |

## Common failure patterns

- "As a user" with no meaningful persona.
- Acceptance criteria missing.
- Acceptance criteria restate the title.
- A story spans too many subsystems.
- Implementation details replace user/business outcomes.
- Major unknowns make estimation impossible; consider a Spike.
